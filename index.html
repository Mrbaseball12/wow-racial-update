Upcoming Changes to Racial Abilities

The Forever Development Team · October 28, 2026

Addressing Class and Racial Balance

Following ongoing discussions regarding class racials and racial advantages, the Forever Development Team is evaluating a significant change to the racial ability system.

To create a fairer competitive environment, all racial abilities are proposed to be temporarily removed from the game while a redesigned system is developed.

Our objective is to ensure that every race can compete on equal footing, with player skill, class mastery, and strategic decisions taking priority over racial selection.

What Players Can Expect

  • A review of existing racial abilities and bonuses.
  • Temporary removal of racial abilities while a new system is developed.
  • A redesigned system focused on competitive fairness for all races.

The Forever Development Team will share further details once the new racial system has been finalized.
